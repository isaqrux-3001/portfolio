Workforce Management

Role: UX Designer

Scheduling and planning employees' work time

01

Human Problem

HR, payroll, talent, and timekeeping - all in one unified system. Deliver a great employee experience throughout the complete employee lifecycle. Imagine having to manage a hospital, a retail store or maybe an attraction park workforce! That is a big amount of employees requesting time off and moving shifts around. It was a great experience helping users by designing an interface that will simplify managers and employees' ways to fulfill their tasks.

02

Scheduling and planning employees' work time

As an ER nurse, I want to publish my work availability so that my employer can update my work schedule

Working as an ER nurse for the Massachussetts General Hospital is great, but sometimes my manager struggle to plan my schedule. I wish I could submit my available time so she can schedule me in advance...

03

My role as a UX Designer

I was in charge of creating the end-to-end flow for the employees who wished to notify their employers what was their availability by using the Kronos app instead of using the traditional ways (phone calls, emails, or simply a written note).

I delivered the wireframes for both mobile and desktop views, along with detailed documentation explaining all needed interactions for the UI team to create the hi-fidelity prototypes that were meant to be delivered for development.
