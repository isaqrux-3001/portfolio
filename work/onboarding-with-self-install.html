Onboarding with Self-Install

Role: Product Designer

Discovering and designing for a new user need enabling customers to set up their own internet without technician assistance through an intuitive mobile app experience.

01

The Problem

Users wanted to save money and gain independence, but self-installation was intimidating. Internet providers offer devices and QR codes, but without guidance, customers often struggled with setup. The challenge: how to deliver a simple, hand-held experience that builds confidence and reduces frustration, covering a variety of modem and router models without overwhelming the user.

02

Context and Constraints

The business needed to support the entire end-to-end journey, starting when a customer decides to self-install, not just when problems arise. Key constraints included:

  • Wide variation in devices and models (including customer-owned equipment).
  • Need for clarity and reassurance without overwhelming users.
  • Scalability for the business while keeping the process simple for customers.

Key Constraint

The app had to guide diverse users through device setup confidently and efficiently while remaining flexible for multiple device types and ISP workflows.

03

Understanding the System

I mapped the user journey from the moment customers received their devices to full setup. Research included:

  • Interviews with customers and domain experts.
  • Observations of common installation pain points.
  • Analysis of workflows across different ISPs and device types.

This helped identify user motivations (convenience, cost savings) and frustration points, highlighting opportunities to simplify the process.

04

Design Process

  • Journey mapping and persona creation: Identified different customer types and their confidence levels.
  • Prototyping: Low, and high-fidelity designs for step-by-step guidance and development handoff.
  • AI integration: Explored image recognition to identify devices automatically, reducing manual input and errors.

05

Key Design Decisions

  • Personalized guidance: Replaced generic instructions with dynamic steps tailored to user devices.
  • AI-powered scanning: Users can scan device labels to auto-populate setup information.
  • Simplified content: Bite-sized instructions and visual cues reduced cognitive load.
  • Flexible workflows: Designed to accommodate different router/modem models and user contexts.

06

Final Solution

The app delivers a seamless self-install experience: Users scan their devices and follow a guided, step-by-step journey. AI automatically identifies equipment, eliminating forms and manual entry. The interface provides reassurance and feedback, helping users feel in control and confident.

07

Impact

  • Increased user adoption of self-installation.
  • Reduced support calls and manual assistance.
  • Reduced truck calls.
  • Enhanced customer satisfaction by providing a guided, almost "magical" experience.

08

Learnings

  • User experience begins before opening the app, from the moment customers interact with the box or instructions.
  • Understanding user emotions (confidence, curiosity, hesitation) is as important as understanding their tasks.
  • AI and automation can simplify complex processes, but clarity and reassurance remain critical.
