Virtual Signing Room

Role: Design Lead, OneSpan Sign

In-person remote signing that includes video, recording & co-browsing capacities.

01

Human Problem

With COVID-19, banks, financial services and insurance companies are jump-starting their digital transformation towards an omnichannel experience. Many of the eSignature use cases, such as Mortgage closing, used to require face-to-face interaction carried out in a branch. They now need to be able to give the same level of assistance in guiding the signer remotely in order to complete the signing of the documents.

02

Business Goals and Metrics

  • Market differentiation: The new feature makes the solution different from the company main competitors
  • Market demand: High demand and expectation from top customers
  • Land and expand: Support new lines of business and use cases from existing customers
  • Monetization: The new feature is offered as an added monetized solution

03

My role as design lead of OneSpan Sign

I designed the research phase to deliver the material the product managers could use to help other stakeholders empathize with the end-user and this way get the needed support to carry on with the feature development. I used insights from previous user interviews that were already in the company database and I created the personas altogether with each persona journey map for the use cases that were defined. Once we got the feature approved, I delivered the hi-fidelity interactive prototype that was used for usability testing and the proper documentation once it moved to the development phase.

04

Outcome after Delivery

  • Increased user retention
  • New customers onboard
  • Old customers moving from the legacy solution to the new OneSpan Signature

05

Tech Challenges

  • The feature had to be integrated into an existing eviroment.
  • Respect 3rd parties UI customization allowance
  • Limited resources to work on development
  • Rebranded (white-label) product

06

Simplify Complex Design

  • Reduce number of visible options to only the most used ones
  • Improve the hierarchy of the information
  • Guide process for the participants
  • Rely not only on the UI but also on verbal communication

07

Video Area Variations

Both, the transaction host and the signer will have a video panel to communicate with each other while the signing ceremony occurs.
