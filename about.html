Eleven years of making complex work feel simple.

01.1
01.2

I’m a product designer with 11+ years in B2B and enterprise software. I studied industrial design in Havana, then Human-Computer Interaction (a DESS in Ergonomics) at Polytechnique de Montréal, and took Stanford’s course on AI and user experience in 2023.

At RouteThis I’m the only product designer. On each product I work in a triad with a Product Manager and a Tech Lead, alongside the project’s developers and the Sales, Customer Experience and Marketing teams. I lead contract UI designers, mentor a co-op developer (and last year a co-op PM), and run discovery sessions, design sprints and Lunch & Learns. From 2021 to 2024 I also mentored UX certificate students at Concordia University.

I speak English, French and Spanish, and I’m learning Portuguese. Outside work you’ll find me playing badminton, running, swimming or lifting weights.
